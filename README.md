# Lexemo

Lexemo is a **flexible language practice tool**, not a game.

It does not try to motivate you with streaks, levels, rankings, or artificial pressure.  
Instead, it gives you **different ways to practice**, and lets you decide **how strict or loose** your study should be.

Think of it as a **personal practice workspace** for memory, recall, and pronunciation.

---

## Philosophy

Most language apps try to *control* how you learn.

Lexemo does the opposite:
- No forced progression
- No punishment for mistakes
- No “correct way” to study

You choose **how** and **when** to practice.  
The app adapts to you — not the other way around.

---

## Core Features

- Flashcard-based practice (question → answer)
- Multiple practice styles (Free, Speed, SRS)
- Pronunciation mode (speech recognition)
- Syllable mode (read one highlighted syllable at a time)
- Transcription mode (romanize or de-romanize a pasted Russian text)
- Reverse mode (see the answer, type the term)
- Optional hints
- Speech recognition for pronunciation practice
- Text-to-speech
- Local-first (everything stored in your browser)
- Import / export decks as JSON
- Multilingual interface (i18n)

## Practice Styles

Lexemo does not have “game modes”.  
It offers **different ways to practice**, depending on your goal.

### Free Mode (Practice Freely)

Free Mode is about **thinking without consequences**.

- No scheduling
- No performance evaluation
- No penalties for mistakes
- Repeat cards as much as you want

This mode is ideal for:
- Exploring new vocabulary
- Learning without pressure
- Reviewing concepts freely
- Warming up before stricter practice

Free Mode exists to give you **full cognitive freedom**.

---

### Speed Mode (Practice with Time)

Speed Mode is not about competition — it’s about **automatic recall**.

- Time-limited answers
- Fast-paced flow
- Focus on fluency instead of perfection

This mode is useful for:
- Training instant recall
- Reducing mental translation
- Improving response speed
- Pronunciation drills under mild pressure

There are no streaks, rankings, or punishments — just rhythm and focus.

---

### SRS Mode (Practice with Scheduling)

SRS Mode is for when you *want* structure.

It uses a **heuristic-based spaced repetition system** to decide:
- Which cards to show
- When to show them again

Performance is estimated based on:
- Response correctness
- Reaction time
- Typing speed

This mode is ideal for:
- Long-term retention
- Consistent study routines
- Reviewing large decks efficiently

SRS is optional.  
You can ignore it completely and still use Lexemo fully.

---

## Pronunciation Practice

Lexemo supports speech input using the Web Speech API:

- Answer cards by speaking instead of typing
- Combine with Free or Speed practice
- Useful for pronunciation and confidence

This feature runs entirely in the browser.

---

## Local-First by Design

- No accounts
- No backend
- No tracking

All data is stored locally using `localStorage`.

You fully own:
- Your decks
- Your progress
- Your study habits

You can export everything at any time.

---

## Who Is Lexemo For?

Lexemo is for people who:
- Prefer tools over games
- Want control over how they study
- Don’t want artificial motivation systems
- Enjoy flexible, self-directed learning

If you’re looking for streaks, levels, or daily pressure, this app is probably not for you — and that’s intentional.
